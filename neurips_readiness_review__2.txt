REMAINING WEAKNESSES (HIGH PRIORITY)

1. Missing Core Control Experiment

* No Europe-to-Europe transfer baseline.
* This is the single biggest vulnerability.

Risk:
Reviewers may interpret results as generic domain shift rather than cultural mismatch.

Required fix:
Train on one subset of European data and test on another subset to establish baseline transfer degradation.

---

2. Missing Ablation Study

Current issue:

* Paper references ablation as future work.
* This weakens confidence in the modeling choices.

Minimum requirement:
Include a table with:

* TF-IDF only
* Sentence embeddings only
* Combined features

---

3. Lack of Visual Evidence

Current issue:

* Evidence is primarily textual and tabular.
* NeurIPS reviewers rely heavily on visual summaries.

Required additions:

* One distribution plot (Western vs Asian entropy)
* One pipeline diagram

---

4. Related Work Still Thin

Current issue:

* Limited engagement with:

  * benchmark auditing literature
  * dataset bias / transferability research
  * cross-domain generalization work

Impact:

* Weakens perceived originality
* Makes contribution seem isolated rather than part of an ongoing ML conversation

---

5. Residual Over-Interpretation

Example:

* Claims about cultural contact influencing transferability are speculative.

Recommended fix:

* Replace causal language with cautious phrasing:

  * “consistent with”
  * “suggestive but not directly tested”
